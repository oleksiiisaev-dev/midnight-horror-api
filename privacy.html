Privacy Policy – Midnight Horror Mods

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Midnight Horror Mods (package com.betterskills.mod.addon.midnighthorror, "the App") lets you browse and install original horror add-ons, maps and skins made by BetterSkills for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. It is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Mojang AB or Microsoft.

Data We Collect

The App has no account and no sign-in, and it does not ask for your name or email. The services below may collect data from your device:

  • Google AdMob — displays ads: a banner at the bottom of the catalog screen, an optional rewarded video before installing a free mod, and an occasional full-screen ad between screens. It may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device model and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Google ads privacy.
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage statistics such as screen views, app opens and which mods are opened.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports (device model, OS version, stack trace) so we can fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config — downloads app settings; it uses an installation identifier.
  • Google Play Billing — the optional Premium subscription (no ads, all mods) is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

See the Firebase privacy information for details about the Firebase services.

Consent

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy Options in the App's Settings. No ads are requested until consent has been handled.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No name, email, contacts, location, photos or other personally identifiable information.
  • No data is sold to third parties.

Mod Downloads and Minecraft Files

Mod files are downloaded from our public GitHub repository (github.com), which receives your IP address as part of any normal download. When you tap Install, the App saves the file in its own private cache and hands it to Minecraft to import. The App does not read or change your other files, and nothing is uploaded to our servers.

Permissions

The App uses Internet and network state access (to load the catalog, mod files and ads), the Google Play billing permission (for the subscription) and the advertising ID permission (used by AdMob).

Children

This app is rated Teen (13+) and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
