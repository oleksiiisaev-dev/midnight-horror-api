Privacy Policy – Hellsaw Hunters Mod for MCPE

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Hellsaw Hunters Mod for MCPE (package com.BetterSkills.Mod.addon.Chainsaw, "the App") installs an original demon-hunter-themed addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. It is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Mojang AB or Microsoft.

Data We Collect

The App itself has no account, no sign-in and no analytics. It does not collect or store any personal data on its own. Ads are provided by third-party services that may collect data from your device:

  • Google AdMob — displays ads (an optional rewarded video before install, and an occasional full-screen ad after an install or when you return to the App). It may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device model and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Google ads privacy.
  • Unity Ads — an ad network that AdMob may use to fill ads (mediation). It may collect similar device and ad interaction data. Unity privacy policy.

Consent

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested, and can change their choice at any time with the "Privacy Options" button on the main screen. No ads are requested until consent has been handled.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No name, email, contacts, location, photos or other personally identifiable information.
  • No data is sold to third parties.

Minecraft Files

When you tap an install button, the App copies the addon file from its own storage into its private folder and hands it to Minecraft to import. The App does not read your other files, and nothing is uploaded to our servers.

Permissions

The App only uses Internet and network state access (to load ads) and the advertising ID permission (used by the ad services above).

Children

This app is rated Teen (13+) and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
